# 20-month strategy search — run 1

Window 2025-01-15 to 2026-09-15. SPY and QQQ, 1-minute SIP bars.
126 configurations across three strategy families. Fitted on the first ~14
months, validated on an untouched ~6-month holdout. 197 seconds of compute.

## Finalists

All five were opening range breakout. The top-N ranking was monopolised by one
family, which run 2 fixes with a per-family cap.

|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| +5.57 | 1.83 | -13.55 | -3.39 | 171 |
| +5.20 | 1.70 | -14.86 | -3.57 | 159 |

## Reading

**Nothing was significant even in-sample.** Every in-sample t-statistic is
below 2, ranging 1.17 to 1.87. These are the winners of a 126-way search,
selected on the very data those numbers come from, and they still fail the
significance bar there. That by itself says the apparent edge is noise.

**The holdout losses are significant in the wrong direction.** t around -3.5
across 154 to 175 trades. These configurations do not merely fail to profit,
they lose reliably. A sign flip from positive in-sample to strongly negative
out-of-sample is the signature of fitting to noise.

**The failure is directional, not cost-driven.** At -14 bps per trade against
a 2.7 bps round-trip cost, costs explain a fifth of the loss at most. The
remainder is the strategy being wrong about direction: opening-range breakouts
failed during the holdout period.

## What it suggests, and the trap in acting on it

If breakouts lost this consistently, the period may have rewarded intraday
reversion instead. That is worth testing, and run 2 does so with a family cap
so VWAP reversion and momentum reach the holdout.

It is **not** a licence to simply invert this strategy and call the result an
edge. The hypothesis "fade the breakout" was generated by looking at holdout
results, so testing it on that same holdout is no longer an out-of-sample
test. Doing that is how a search talks itself into a strategy that dies with
real money. Any inverted idea needs a fresh validation period or different
instruments.

## Verdict

Do not execute any configuration in this table.
